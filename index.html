AI will touch every career in this room.

Here's how to be the person using it well — not the person replaced by it. Notes from Brother Knowles, software engineer.

1 · Build your foundation

Know your stuff without the AI.

  • Learn your field deeply. AI is a tool that helps you get things done, but you still need to understand what's going on. Keep building your own knowledge without it.
  • Become a strong reader. You'll have to understand what the AI is doing — and correct it when it's wrong. That takes real reading comprehension.
  • Learn to search. Knowing how to find good information yourself is still a superpower.
2 · Pick a direction and start building

Aim somewhere specific. Then make something.

  • Choose a focus. Enter your field with a specific area in mind. A clear goal gives you a path to follow.
  • Start building. Your plan doesn't have to be perfect. As you work — especially with AI — something real starts to take shape.
  • Get to know people. Be genuine and bring something to the table. Reach out often, and offer to help for free to get your foot in the door.
3 · Keep growing

Change faster on the inside than the world changes on the outside.

  • Embrace change. The tools will keep changing. If you keep learning faster than they change, you'll always be ready.
